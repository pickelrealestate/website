# Pickel Sells ATL Design Direction

## Mood

An Atlanta house tour just after golden hour: deep blue shadows, warm porch lights, leafy streets, and a person who makes the next step feel easy.

## Color

- Background: `oklch(0.985 0 0)` — pure, bright search-friendly canvas.
- Ink: `oklch(0.18 0.025 262)` — deep blue-black for 7:1+ body contrast.
- Primary: `oklch(0.34 0.159 262.4)` — cobalt twilight.
- Accent: `oklch(0.76 0.145 68)` — warm porch-light amber.
- Surface: `oklch(0.95 0.012 262)` — cool blue-gray section surface.

## Typography

Use Georgia for the human, residential voice of display headings and a system sans stack for body copy, labels, and buttons. Headings should be fluid and balanced; body copy should stay around 65–75 characters per line.

## Layout & Motion

Full-bleed architectural photography, sharp framed image windows, and a generous max-width content rail. Motion is limited to low-cost transforms and opacity, with all content visible before animation and a reduced-motion override.
