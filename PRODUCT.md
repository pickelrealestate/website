# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Atlanta-area home buyers, sellers, and people considering a move who want a calm, direct conversation with Bobby before making a major real-estate decision.

## Product Purpose

Turn local search attention into qualified conversations. The site should make Bobby feel easy to reach, show real proof through the supplied listing photography and videos, and help a visitor choose a next step for buying, selling, or viewing a home.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, observant, unhurried. The voice should feel like a trusted neighbor who knows the market and gives straight answers without pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic luxury real-estate clichés, aggressive countdowns, vague claims of being “the best,” stock-photo overload, tiny unreadable labels, and a polished-but-anonymous brokerage feel.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a human face and a simple next step.
- Let real homes and real Atlanta context do the convincing.
- Use clarity as a trust signal: explain what happens after a visitor reaches out.
- Make every important action reachable on a phone and with a keyboard.
- Use motion to create a sense of place, never to hide information or slow the page down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve readable contrast, visible focus, semantic landmarks, labeled forms, meaningful alternative text, keyboard access, and a reduced-motion experience.
